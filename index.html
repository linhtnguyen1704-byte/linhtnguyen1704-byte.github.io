One Small Habit Bot

One Small Habit Bot is the channel owner's own publishing tool. It runs on the owner's computer and only acts on the owner's own accounts. It uploads and schedules the channel's videos on YouTube, adds captions, reads view and audience statistics for those videos, keeps a progress dashboard in the owner's Google Sheet, and emails the owner when a scheduled upload fails. It is not offered to other users.

How the bot handles Google user data is described in the Privacy Policy. Use of the bot is covered by the Terms of Service.

The videos are for general education only and are not medical advice.
