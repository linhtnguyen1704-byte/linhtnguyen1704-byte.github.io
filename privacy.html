Privacy Policy

One Small Habit Bot · Last updated 26 October 2026

One Small Habit Bot ("the app") is a private publishing tool run by the owner of the One Small Habit channels (contact: linht.nguyen1704@gmail.com). It runs on the owner's own computer and signs in only to the owner's own Google accounts and YouTube channels. It has no other users. This policy explains which Google user data the app accesses and what it does with it.

Google user data the app accesses

  • YouTube (scopes youtube.upload, youtube.readonly, youtube.force-ssl): upload and schedule the channel's own videos, set their titles and descriptions, add caption tracks, and read the channel's videos and their public statistics (views, likes, comments).
  • YouTube Analytics (scopes yt-analytics.readonly, yt-analytics-monetary.readonly): read audience retention, subscribers gained and estimated revenue for the channel's own videos.
  • Google Sheets (scope spreadsheets): write the channel's statistics into one dashboard spreadsheet owned by the channel owner.
  • Gmail (scope gmail.send): send alert emails to the owner's own address when a scheduled upload or check fails. The app does not read, search or delete any email.

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish the channel's videos, to track how those videos perform, and to tell the owner which topics viewers find useful so future videos can improve. The app does not use the data for advertising, does not build profiles of viewers, and does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens and the collected statistics are stored only on the owner's computer, in files readable by the owner's account. Statistics are also written to the owner's own Google Sheet. Nothing is stored on any other server run by the app.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone. It is sent only to Google's own APIs (YouTube, YouTube Analytics, Sheets, Gmail) to carry out the actions above.

Limited Use

One Small Habit Bot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app's use of YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept for as long as the channels are active, so growth can be compared over time. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the owner can delete the local token and statistics files at any time, and on request any stored data will be deleted within 30 days.

Viewers of the videos

The app does not collect personal information about people who watch the videos. YouTube and Facebook process viewer data under their own privacy policies.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: linht.nguyen1704@gmail.com.
